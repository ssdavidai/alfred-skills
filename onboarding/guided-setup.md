# Guided setup

The nine-step setup, for an owner who is not doing the 7-day challenge. If they say "I'm doing the 7-day challenge", switch to `challenge.md` and the day files. Ask one question at a time. Say "pause" whenever they like; "resume" picks up where you stopped.

## Step 1: Who you are
1. What should I call you, and what's your time zone?
2. What's your role? For example founder, operator, consultant.
3. Who's in your household that briefings should know about? Roles only, or first names you're happy for me to use.
4. Preferences: dark or light visuals, language, and how short messages should be.
Checkpoint: I read your profile back and save it.

## Step 2: Connections
One at a time: email, calendar, files, chat (e.g. Slack), meeting notes, and your CRM. Connect one if this assistant has that connector. Otherwise they paste. Test with one harmless read (latest email subject, tomorrow's first event). Failures are noted and skipped. Do not invent a connector.
Checkpoint: connected / failed / skipped.

## Step 3: Map your life areas
5. List your areas: each venture or client, family and household, side projects.
6. For each: which sources belong to it, who's involved (roles only), and its working language.
Then I propose one teammate assistant per area where it's worth it, using the area-bot-brief skill: scope, sources, what it may send on its own (by default nothing), and its rituals. I write the brief. You create the assistant, if you want it, only after you approve the brief. I stay your front door.
Checkpoint: the area map and any bots created.

## Step 4: Trackers per area
7. Which areas get a Work sheet (item, owner, due, status, source link) and an Hours sheet (time per day, rebuilt from calendar plus evidence)?
Prefer a copy of the Matter Ledger sheet. If they cannot use it, use the markdown ledger in this pack (`templates/ledger.md`) with the same three tables. Save the link or the file name, and use work-tracker, hours-tracker and tracker-dry-run. I seed each with one proposed catch-up for you to approve.
8. The nightly pass proposes changes by default; you reply "apply" to write. Want auto-apply for any area? It never overwrites your manual edits.
Checkpoint: sheets created and the mode for each.

## Step 5: Your week's shape
This feeds the weekly calendar build (weekly-planning skill).
9. Fixed points: recurring appointments, school runs, family rhythms, travel.
10. Work hours: when does your working day start and stop, and which days?
11. Lunch: when and how long?
12. Resets: a 15-minute reset after each demanding block (default), or another length? Max two demanding blocks a day unless you say otherwise.
13. Movement: when and how often?
14. End of day: a hard stop time?
15. Weekly me-time: which slot is yours and protected?
Checkpoint: I read back your week's shape, save it to `calendar.md`, and propose a first week as a day-by-day list. Events go on the Butler calendar only, and only after you say "apply".

## Step 6: Calls
16. Do you want meeting prep before named calls, and a deliverables list after calls (drafts only)?
Checkpoint: saved.

## Step 7: Routines
17. Times for: morning briefing (default 07:50), evening heads-up (21:00), weekly calendar build (Sunday 18:00), nightly tracker pass (22:30, propose only).
18. Optional: a nudge when each Butler block starts. Use the calendar-block-interrupts ritual. Do not require a computer registered to you.
Checkpoint: each ritual is registered with their times, and they confirm the list. Schedule only where the product can.

## Step 8: First briefing
I run your first morning briefing right now, so you see what it looks like and can tune length and order.

## Step 9: Recap and handover
A recap of everything: profile, connections, areas, bots, sheets, week shape, routines and times. Then the trust ladder: everything starts propose-only; you can loosen specific things later. I mark onboarding complete.
