### Day 3. Connections and first briefing (onboarding Steps 2 and 8)

Input: run the tools-interview skill first (it saves `my-tools.md`); an attached or pasted summary, or the short-version questions, also work. The owner does the clicking; you do the thinking.

**Part A: connections**
1. **Read it back**, one line per tool: `priority · tool · matters · access · method you'll try first`. Map the list onto the standard checklist (email, calendar, Drive, Slack, meeting notes such as Fathom, CRM). For each standard item that's missing, ask once: "connect, or skip?" Apply the corrections.
2. **Test what's already connected first** (`access.md` from Day 1). Don't make the owner reconnect something that already works.
3. **For each "Connect now" tool, in priority order:** (a) a connector this assistant already has: tell them where this product keeps connectors, make sure it's the right account, and wait while they sign in; (b) else an app or catalog connection this product actually lists: walk them through authorising it; (c) else they paste a harmless sample or an export; (d) else skip, record why, and suggest a workaround (a forwarding rule, a weekly export, or pasting the key bits). Never ask them to paste a password, code, or recovery key. Do not claim a built-in browser or a saved-login vault unless this session has one. Grok Bot does; Claude and ChatGPT do not, unless the owner is in a mode that really has a browser, in which case they type the password themselves. Then run a **read-only smoke test**: one harmless item (latest subject, tomorrow's first event, one file or channel name), with nothing opened, quoted or marked read. If it fails, show the exact error and try the next method. Record each tool before you move on.

4. **Access levels**, saved to memory per tool. **Read:** look only, never change, move, label, archive or mark read. **Draft:** drafts and proposals only, the owner sends. **Write, own space:** only your own calendar, sheets, folder or channel. **Write for others:** ask every time in chat, with the exact content, and there's no standing permission. **Never connect:** don't connect, open or log in, even if asked in passing, and remind them they chose this. Banking and password managers are never connected.
5. **Source map:** write `sources.md` with one section per matter, listing every source under each matter it serves (`[tool] ([account]): [senders, labels, channels, folders, calendar names] · access: [level] · status: [connected / pasted / skipped: why]`), plus `## Coverage gaps` (with workarounds, e.g. "forward important WhatsApp bits to email") and `## Never connect`. Be specific, because the Day 5 trackers read their sources from this file. Add a `Sources` line to each `matters-[CODE].md`.
6. **Teammate bots:** propose granting each one only its own matter's connections, at the same access level or lower, and only on the owner's yes.
7. **Pace:** "Later" tools don't get connected today. If time runs short, connect the top three, then go to Part B.

**Part B: briefing routines**
8. **Morning briefing**, every day at [07:50] in the owner's time zone [weekdays only unless they say otherwise], in this chat. Read-only. Until Day 5 it reads the calendars, each matter file's Open section, and `promises.md`; from Day 5 it also reads the ledgers.
9. **Evening heads-up** at [21:00]: tomorrow's first commitments, anything due, anything to prepare tonight, in under 8 lines. Offer to fold in the promise question and retire the separate Nightly promise log, and do it only on the owner's yes.
10. Read both routines back (owner, time, time zone, does, never does) and get approval. Then **run the first briefing now** and ask: "Shorter, longer, or a different order?" Save the answer as a briefing preference.
