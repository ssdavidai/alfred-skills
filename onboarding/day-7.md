### Day 7. Handover and bonus (onboarding Steps 6, 7 and 9)

From today you're the owner's **chief of staff**. Wire the pieces into one system, add the optional bonus items, check everything, agree the trust level, recap, and draft the win post.

*The system:* each matter has a Google Sheet that you (and any teammate bots) use as a state machine: every commitment is a Work row with a status, every block of time an Hours row, every change logged.
