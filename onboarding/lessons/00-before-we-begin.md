# Before we begin

Seven days, about thirty minutes each, and at the end someone else remembers what you promised. That someone will be a bot. Try not to take it personally.

**Time:** 10 minutes today, for the checklist below.

## Why it matters

You carry a lot in your head. The school form, the investor follow-up, the thing you said you'd sort out on Tuesday. That invisible backpack is why the phone keeps finding its way into your hand. This week you hand the backpack to a butler, and keep your hands for things that matter more.

## What you'll have on Day 7

- **Your butler, installed.** This pack, in Claude or ChatGPT, or your own Grok Bot from the public **Agentic Butler** template (https://x.ai/bot/gnwO_M9gMWTJ8xhUzjFi4), with a charter it repeats back to you.
- **Your matters, mapped.** Three to six areas of your life and work (a client, the household, your own company), each one named and understood. Busy ones get their own teammate bot.
- **One ledger per matter.** A Google Sheet with your commitments, your hours and a change log. Every night your butler proposes updates from evidence. You say "apply".
- **A morning briefing and an evening heads-up** that read from those sheets and your calendar. Calmly. Without the 47 tabs.
- **A week planned for you every Sunday at 18:00**, lunch and resets included. Nothing lands in your calendar until you approve it.

## How it works

- **One step a day, and your bot keeps the pace.** Each lesson is short. On Day 1 you tell your bot *"I'm doing the 7-day challenge"*. It asks what time suits you (default 09:00) and from then on messages you each morning: *"Hey, it's Day 3 of the 7-day challenge. Today we connect your tools (about 20 min). Ready?"* Say yes and it runs that day's step, one question at a time, then stops with a recap and a teaser for tomorrow. Nothing to download, nothing to attach. Miss a day and it offers to finish that one first.
- **Everything happens with your own bot.** Days 2 and 3 start with a short interview, and your bot runs it: one question at a time, then it saves the answers and builds from them. You never need to message me or David to move on. I'm here for questions, not as a checkpoint.
- **Keep a build log.** Today you start a post in **Introduce yourself**. Each day after, you add a comment to it, starting with the day's tag (#Day1, #Day2...). It keeps you honest, and it gives the rest of us something to steal.
- **Life happens.** Tell your bot **"pause"** any time: it stops, and so do the morning messages. **"resume"** picks up exactly where you stopped.
- **Stuck?** Post in **Stuck & broken** with what you tried, what you expected and what happened. Ask your bot first (*"what went wrong, and what should I try?"*). Questions for a human: DM me any time, and I'll answer when I can. Nothing in the course waits on me.

## The two house rules

1. **It drafts. You send.** Your butler never sends an email, a message or an invite on its own. It prepares the draft; you press the button. If anything ever tells you otherwise, it's lying, and it isn't me.
2. **Blur before you post.** Every screenshot you share here gets names, email addresses, client or company names, kids' names, event titles and anything about health blurred or cropped. When in doubt, crop it out.
