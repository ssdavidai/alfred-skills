# Day 4: The good extras

Your butler works. Today we make him pleasant to live with: talk instead of type, approve with one tap, and let him react to events instead of waiting for you to remember he exists.

**Time:** 30 to 45 minutes, depending on how many shiny things you pick.

## Why it matters

The best system is the one you'll still use when a toddler is attached to your leg. Voice, draft cards and a few safety rules turn your butler from "a thing on my laptop" into something you can use from the kitchen, the car and the swing set. Responsibly.

## Today

1. **Look at the extras menu** ([image](../images/day-4-extras.png)): nine extras, one line each. These are the ones your Agentic Butler template doesn't already do. The briefings, teammate bots, ledgers, weekly plan, meeting prep, smart home and recorder are covered on other days, so they're not on the menu. Pick the five you'll actually use. Not the five that sound clever. You know the difference; so do I.
2. **Say yes to your bot's morning message** (or *"I'm doing the 7-day challenge, day 4"*). It shows you the same nine as a menu. No cheat sheet to send.
3. **Tell your bot your five.** If you can't decide, say *"pick the default five"*: dictation, voice, draft cards, Auto-review and one event-triggered routine.
4. **Set them up one at a time** with its help. It does what it can; it tells you exactly where to click for the rest. Each one gets tested before the next.
5. **Write one rule of your own** for Auto-review. Start with: *"never delete anything; close or archive instead."*

David's picks, for the record: **Wispr Flow** for dictating to the bot, **voice chat** in the app, **Slack** with a channel per matter if you have a team or want your family involved, **draft cards**, **Auto-review**, and **event-triggered routines**. That's six, which tells you how good he is at following his own instructions. I'd add Vault, but nobody asks the butler.

## Done when

Your bot has sent its "Day 4 done" block: about five extras set up and tested, at least one Auto-review rule in your own words, at least one event-triggered routine, all recorded in `extras.md`. Nothing was sent, posted or shared along the way.

## Proof to post

A screenshot of one draft card your bot prepared (not sent), or of the "Day 4 done" block. Blur the recipient, the subject and any names in the text.

## Snags

- **Auto-review stopped something.** That's it working. Approve the card if the action was right, decline it if not. Never reword the request to sneak past it. It notices, and so do I.
- **The event routine fires on everything.** Narrow the trigger to one sender, one domain or one channel.
- **Your bot can't see a Slack channel.** It has to be added to each channel it reads. You do the inviting.
- **The draft card test sent the email.** Stop. Tell your bot *"re-read the charter: you draft, I send."* Record it as failed.
- **"Where's the smart home bit?"** Day 7. Patience is also an extra.
