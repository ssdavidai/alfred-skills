# Day 7: Hand over the keys

Six days of parts. Today it becomes one system: a chief of staff you talk to, a ledger per matter that stays true with your nod, and a briefing every morning that reads from them. Plus a few bonus toys. I'd get emotional, but I'm British in spirit.

**Time:** 30 to 45 minutes, plus tomorrow morning's coffee.

## Why it matters

A system you don't trust is a system you check, and checking is what we're trying to get you out of. Today you agree exactly how much your butler may do on his own, and how he earns more. Then you can put the phone face down at dinner and mean it.

## Today

1. **Say yes to your bot's last morning message** (or *"I'm doing the 7-day challenge, day 7"*). When you finish, it congratulates you and switches the morning messages off.
2. **Make it your chief of staff.** One bot you talk to about everything, from the app on desktop and phone, or Slack. Teammate bots from Day 2 report into the same ledgers; your chief of staff briefs you. Any bot still "proposed": create it from its brief, or let it go.
3. **Agree the trust ladder.** Read, then draft and propose, then act in its own space, then act outward only under a rule you've written. It earns each rung over two weeks. You promote; it doesn't. Auto-applying the nightly ledger pass is a promotion too, one matter at a time.
4. **Let it point your briefings at the ledgers.** The 07:50 morning briefing now reads every sheet and tells you how many proposals are waiting. The 21:00 heads-up stays short.
5. **The bonus round. All optional.** Say yes or no to each:
    - **Meeting prep** before named calls, and a **deliverables list** after calls. Follow-ups come as draft cards. You send.
    - **Mac full-screen nudges** when each Butler block starts, if you use a Mac.
    - **Home Assistant**: its address and a long-lived token, stored as a secret. Never paste the token in chat. One read-only test; it changes nothing without asking.
    - **A wearable recorder**, so promises you make out loud turn up as proposals in the right ledger.
6. **Run the self-check, read the recap, post your win.** Your bot checks everything from Days 1 to 6 actually works, warns you about any active matter still without a ledger, tests that real things made it into your sheets, gives you the full recap and drafts your post. You add the screenshot and press post. Yes, you. Draft before send, remember?

## The end state

- **K matters, K ledgers.** Every active matter in `matters.md` has a live ledger; anything not ready sits under Later. Each sheet is the state of that part of your life. Every night your butler proposes updates from evidence; you apply them.
- **One chief of staff** who knows all of it, reachable from your pocket, with teammate bots for the busy matters if you want them.
- **A morning briefing and an evening heads-up** that work from the sheets, not from vibes.
- **A week proposed every Sunday**, resets included.
- **The sheets capture everything relevant.** When something real happens and isn't in a sheet, that's a hole, and your bot's job is to find it.

## Snags

- **The briefing only talks about the calendar.** It isn't reading the ledgers. *"Re-run the Day 7 briefing step: point the briefings at the ledgers."*
- **The self-check says FIX.** Fix in day order. Day 3 before Day 5. Ask your bot *"explain this FIX and walk me through it"*. Still unclear? Post the FIX line in **Stuck & broken**.
- **You pasted the Home Assistant token in chat.** Revoke it in Home Assistant, make a new one, use the secret prompt. No harm done if you're quick.
- **The recorder catches everything.** *"Only suggest clear 'I will…' commitments or ones with a date."*
- **"Can it just auto-apply now?"** In two weeks, one matter at a time, with evidence. Day 7 is the start of trust, not the end.
