# Day 5: Your ledgers

**[Make a copy of the Matter Ledger](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15ikRHneBDL3v1ixltMG_GHxNI9km0WicvIKZMnRVIBg/copy)**, one per matter. Then say yes to your bot's morning message and paste the links when it asks.

That's the lesson. The rest is me explaining why it works, because I'm a butler and explaining is half the job.

**Time:** 10 minutes of copying, then 30 to 45 with your bot.

## Why it matters

Most dad guilt is a promise that slipped. "I'll send the deck." "I'll book swimming." A ledger catches every one, with a date and a link to where you said it, so nothing depends on your memory at 23:00. And because your butler only proposes changes until you say "apply", you stay the one who decides what's true.

## Today

1. **Copy the template once per matter.** Name each copy `[CODE] · [Matter name] · Ledger`, e.g. `CLA · Client A · Ledger`. Short on time? Start with your two first-wave matters. By Day 7, every active matter has a live ledger or sits under Later in `matters.md`. Day 7 checks.
2. **Say yes to this morning's message and send the links.** (Missed it? *"I'm doing the 7-day challenge, day 5."*) Your bot already has the tracker skills from the template (work-tracker, hours-tracker, tracker-dry-run). It attaches them to your ledgers and maps their columns. No files to send at all.
3. **Read the dry run.** Your bot backfills the last 14 days without writing anything and shows you what it would add, including your Day 1 promises and Day 2 open items. Is every row sourced? Did it invent a deadline? Be picky. It can take it.
4. **Say "apply".** Only when the dry run is right. Corrections first, then apply.
5. **Approve the nightly pass.** Every night at 22:30 your bot **proposes** updates for every ledger. It writes nothing. In the morning your briefing says "3 proposals waiting"; you reply *"apply"*, *"apply CLA"*, or pick rows. Optional: a short Friday review at 16:00.

## Why a sheet and not "the bot remembers"

Memory is for preferences. Commitments need a ledger: one row per promise, a status, a date, and a link to the email where you said it. Every write is logged. Nothing is ever deleted, only closed with a reason. When the bot is wrong, you can see exactly where and fix it. Try that with vibes.

Each ledger has three tabs you'll actually look at:

- **Work**: what you owe, to whom, by when, and what state it's in
- **Hours**: your time on the matter, reconstructed from evidence in quarter hours. No timers. No questionnaires. You're welcome.
- **Change log**: every change your bot made, and why

## Done when

Your bot has sent its "Day 5 done" block: ledgers live for at least your two first-wave matters, backfill applied after your "apply", tracker skills attached, nightly pass set to **propose**, registry written.

## Proof to post

A screenshot of one Work tab with the header row visible and every cell below it blurred. The number of forgotten commitments it found goes in words.

## Snags

- **The copy link says "request access".** Are you signed in to Google in that browser? If you are and it still asks, ask your bot *"why does this link say request access?"* with the exact message. Still stuck: post it in **Stuck & broken**.
- **The copy landed in the wrong Google account.** Copy again while signed in to the account your bot uses, or share the copy with it as an editor.
- **"Header missing or renamed".** Someone tidied. (You.) Undo the rename, or start a fresh copy.
- **The dry run invented a deadline.** *"That row has no agreed date, use —."* Real dates only.
- **"Can't it just update the sheets itself?"** Not yet. Two weeks of proposals you'd have applied as-is, then you can promote one matter to auto-apply on Day 7's trust ladder. Trust is earned. Even by spreadsheets.
