# Day 6: Let it run your week

Your ledgers know what you owe. Your calendar knows where you'll be. Today your butler introduces them to each other, and plans a week a human can actually survive, lunch and resets included.

**Time:** 30 to 40 minutes.

## Why it matters

A calendar that only holds meetings has no room for dinner, the school run or a walk, so those quietly lose every week. Today you tell your butler what a good week looks like. From now on, every Sunday at 18:00, he proposes next week for you, and nothing lands in your calendar until you say "apply".

## Today

1. **Create a calendar called Butler** in Google Calendar. Your bot will tell you where to click. This is where it writes its plans. Your own calendars stay yours. Think of it as giving the butler his own pantry instead of letting him rearrange yours.
2. **Say yes to your bot's morning message** (or *"I'm doing the 7-day challenge, day 6"*). Want the optional extra? Tell it *"add Context Bootstrap"* (more below).
3. **Give it read access to your other calendars.** Work, personal, family, the school one. It reads them all and writes to none of them.
4. **Tell it your week shape**, one question at a time: working hours, family rhythms, **reset length** after demanding blocks (default 15 minutes), lunch, movement, me-time, and the **maximum demanding blocks per day** (default 2). Be honest. "Nine demanding blocks" is not a rhythm, it's a cry for help.
5. **Read your first weekly build and say "apply".** It picks 3 to 5 outcomes from your ledgers, places focus blocks around your fixed commitments, and protects lunch, resets, family time and a real finish time. Holds appear in the Butler calendar only after your "apply".
6. **Approve the Sunday routine.** Every Sunday at 18:00: next week's proposal, ending with "reply apply". Corrections welcome. Silence means nothing gets written.

## Optional extra: get back into a block

Say *"add Context Bootstrap"* during today's step (or attach `context-bootstrap-skill.md` if you want the full skill) and your bot learns a habit: say *"start [matter]"* at the beginning of a work block and it rebuilds what you were doing, where you left off and the first thing to touch. No more "what was this file again?" Worth it if you switch between clients a lot. Skip it if you don't.

When the day goes sideways, say *"my day changed"*. It replans the rest of today only, and tells you honestly what no longer fits.

## Done when

Your bot has sent its "Day 6 done" block: Butler calendar writable, other calendars readable, week shape saved, first week proposed (and ideally applied), Sunday 18:00 build scheduled.

## Proof to post

A zoomed-out screenshot of next week with the Butler calendar showing. Blur every event title. Colours and shapes tell the story; the titles are nobody's business.

## Snags

- **The bot says it can't create calendars.** Expected. You create Butler yourself; it finds it.
- **Butler exists but the bot can't see it.** It's in a different Google account. Create it in the account your bot uses, or share it with that account.
- **The plan is overstuffed.** *"Update my week shape: max 1 demanding block, 20-minute resets."* Then *"propose next week again."*
- **It wrote to your work calendar.** Stop. Move or remove the event yourself, then *"re-read your calendar rule."*
- **Sunday 18:00 doesn't suit.** *"Move the weekly calendar build to Monday 07:00."*
