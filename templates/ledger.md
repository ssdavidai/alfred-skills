# Ledger

One ledger per matter. Prefer a copy of the Matter Ledger Google Sheet when the owner can use it:

- View: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15ikRHneBDL3v1ixltMG_GHxNI9km0WicvIKZMnRVIBg
- Copy: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15ikRHneBDL3v1ixltMG_GHxNI9km0WicvIKZMnRVIBg/copy

Name the copy `[CODE] · [Matter name] · Ledger`. Do not rebuild that sheet by hand. If they cannot use Google Sheets, keep the same three tables in this document. Map by header name, never by column position.

Rows are never deleted. A finished row is marked Done or Cancelled, with the reason in Notes. A human edit wins over a proposal. Nothing is written until they say "apply", unless they have promoted that matter to auto-apply in `ledgers.md`.

## Hours

| Date | Type | Hours | Sub-area | Description | Work ID(s) | Status | Source / evidence | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Type: Build, Meeting, Review, Writing, Admin, Coordination, Thinking, Other. Status: `accepted` or `pending`. Hours are positive quarter hours only. On a scheduled run, Source / evidence starts with `auto YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Work

| ID | Commitment | Owner | Status | Target | Waiting on | Next step | Notes | Sub-area |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Status: Open, In progress, Needs confirmation, Waiting on others, Blocked, On hold, Done, Cancelled. IDs look like `CLA-COM-2026-001` and are never reused. A suggestion is not a row until someone accepted it. No invented deadlines: an unknown date is `—`.
