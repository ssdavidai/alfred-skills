# Example matters (fiction)

This is a made-up person: a freelance product designer in Berlin, two retainer clients called Client A and Client B, a partner, and two school-age children. Nobody in this file is real. Do not copy it into a real setup. It only shows the shape of a finished `matters.md`.

# My matters: how to set yourself up around my life

You are my butler. On Day 2 of "Build Your Alfred in 7 Days" I mapped the areas of my life and work that keep generating obligations. Each one is a **matter**. From now on you organise everything you do for me around these matters: what you remember, where you file things, which sheet a commitment goes into, and how you brief me.

## About me

- **Scope:** both
- **Time zone:** Europe/Berlin
- **Work shape:** freelance product designer, two retainer clients, a one-person company I run myself
- **Home shape:** partner, two school-age kids
- **Platform:** any assistant
- **I want the time back for:** deep design work in the mornings, and being done by 17:30 for the kids

## My matters

### CLA · Client A

- **What it is:** design retainer with a software company, the biggest client
- **Type:** work
- **First wave (Day 5):** yes
- **Recurring:**
  - Monday design review call (weekly)
  - Friday progress note to their product lead (weekly)
  - Retainer invoice (monthly, first working day)
- **I tend to drop:** the follow-up after Monday's call: I say "I'll send the revised flows" and then don't write it down
- **Who depends on me here:** their product lead, their two developers
- **Open right now:**
  - Product lead · revised onboarding flows · Thursday
  - Developers · exported icon set · —
- **Evidence lives in:** work Gmail, Client A Slack workspace, meeting recorder notes, work calendar
- **Done looks like:** files delivered in their Slack AND the product lead has acknowledged them
- **Sub-areas:** Onboarding, Design system, Meetings
- **Track hours:** yes
- **Working language:** English
- **Teammate bot:** suggest (own Slack workspace, weekly calls)

### ADM · Studio admin

- **What it is:** running my own one-person company: invoicing, the accountant, tax, contracts, subscriptions
- **Type:** work
- **First wave (Day 5):** yes
- **Recurring:**
  - Invoices to both clients (monthly)
  - Send receipts to the accountant (monthly, by the 10th)
  - VAT return (quarterly)
  - Insurance and software renewals (yearly)
- **I tend to drop:** receipts for the accountant; I find them all on the 9th at 23:00
- **Who depends on me here:** the accountant
- **Open right now:**
  - Accountant · last month's receipts · the 10th
  - (to confirm) · contract renewal with Client B · —
- **Evidence lives in:** work Gmail, invoicing tool, Drive folder "Admin"
- **Done looks like:** the accountant confirms receipt; the invoice is sent (payment tracked separately, by me)
- **Sub-areas:** Invoicing, Accountant, Tax, Contracts
- **Track hours:** yes
- **Working language:** English
- **Teammate bot:** no

### CLB · Client B

- **What it is:** smaller monthly retainer, mostly email, occasional workshop
- **Type:** work
- **First wave (Day 5):** no
- **Recurring:**
  - Monthly check-in call (monthly)
  - Workshop prep (when booked, about every 2 months)
- **I tend to drop:** their emails, because they arrive in a busy inbox and never feel urgent until they are
- **Who depends on me here:** their founder
- **Open right now:**
  - Founder · workshop outline · end of month
- **Evidence lives in:** work Gmail, work calendar
- **Done looks like:** outline sent and a workshop date agreed
- **Sub-areas:** Workshops, Retainer
- **Track hours:** yes
- **Working language:** English
- **Teammate bot:** no

### HOME · Household

- **What it is:** running the house with my partner: bills, repairs, renewals, the big shop
- **Type:** life
- **First wave (Day 5):** no
- **Recurring:**
  - Big grocery shop (weekly, Saturday)
  - Bin day (weekly, Tuesday evening)
  - Utility and phone bills (monthly)
  - Car service and home insurance (yearly)
- **I tend to drop:** things I agree to with my partner in passing ("I'll call the plumber")
- **Who depends on me here:** my partner
- **Open right now:**
  - Partner · book the plumber for the bathroom tap · this week
- **Evidence lives in:** personal Gmail, family shared calendar, family chat
- **Done looks like:** the appointment is booked and in the family calendar
- **Sub-areas:** Bills, Repairs, Shopping
- **Track hours:** no
- **Working language:** English
- **Teammate bot:** no

### KIDS · Kids & school

- **What it is:** the kids' school year, clubs and the paperwork that comes with them
- **Type:** life
- **First wave (Day 5):** no
- **Recurring:**
  - School forms and trip payments (as they arrive, about weekly)
  - Swimming club, Wednesday pickup (weekly)
  - Parent evenings (termly)
  - Kids' birthdays and their friends' parties (as they come)
- **I tend to drop:** forms that arrive in the school app; I see them the night before
- **Who depends on me here:** the kids, my partner
- **Open right now:**
  - School · trip consent form · Friday
- **Evidence lives in:** school app notifications in personal Gmail, family shared calendar
- **Done looks like:** form submitted and the event in the family calendar
- **Sub-areas:** School, Clubs, Parties
- **Track hours:** no
- **Working language:** English
- **Teammate bot:** no, for now

## Across all matters

- **Attention leaks:**
  - checking the work inbox in the evening "just to see" if Client A replied
  - re-reading Client A's Slack to remember what was agreed on Monday
  - two calendars (work and family) that don't know about each other
- **Never touch:** my partner's email or accounts; anything in the banking app
- **Read only:** Client A's Slack, the family shared calendar
- **Only I do:** design work itself, client calls, anything with the kids
- **Later:** a side project (a small design course) once the first five run themselves
