# Rituals the challenge adds

These are not the four standing rituals. The day scripts create them. Same rules: a phrase always works, a schedule only when the product can and they have said yes, and a task that cannot see their files must carry its own steps.

## 7-day challenge kickoff

Created on Day 1, retired at the end of Day 7. Phrase: "challenge kickoff". The script is in `onboarding/challenge.md`. It sends one line and waits. It never starts the day on its own.

## Nightly promise log

Created on Day 1. Phrase: "what did I promise today". One question. Append answers to `promises.md`. No date means `—`. Never invent a deadline. Never contact the person they named. Day 3 may fold this into the evening heads-up. Retire this one only if they agree.

## Nightly tracker pass

Created on Day 5. Default 22:30. Phrase: "nightly pass". Follow tracker-dry-run. Mode is propose. Put the proposal where the morning briefing will see it. Do not ping at 22:30 unless they asked for a ping. "apply", "apply CLA", or named rows are the only writes.

## Friday review

Optional, default yes, Fridays at 16:00. Phrase: "friday review". Read-only, under 200 words: per matter, open, overdue, waiting, needs confirmation, hours this week. Up to five items that need them, with IDs. Up to three questions you skipped as ambiguous.
