# Weekly calendar build

One of the four standing rituals from the Agentic Butler template.

- **Trigger phrases:** "plan my week", "weekly calendar", "propose next week"
- **Default time:** Sunday 18:00 in the time zone in `profile.md`.
- **Skill:** weekly-planning.
- **Writes:** the Butler calendar only, and only after they say "apply". Never another calendar. Never an invitation.

Schedule it only after they confirm. If the product cannot schedule, Sunday is a reminder they can ignore, and the phrase still works any day.

## Steps

1. Read every calendar you are allowed to read, the open Work rows, and the deadlines. An unreadable calendar is a gap. Say so.
2. Lay out fixed points first: appointments, family, travel.
3. Then protected blocks: lunch, a reset after each demanding block (default 15 minutes), movement, a clear end of day, and weekly me-time. Use the week shape in `calendar.md`.
4. Then focus blocks for the week's outcomes. At most two demanding blocks a day, unless they changed that number. Work backward from deadlines.
5. Post the proposed week as a short day-by-day list, under 300 words, and end with "Reply apply to put it in your Butler calendar."
6. Only after "apply", create the events on the Butler calendar, read them back, and confirm. On a partial yes, create only those.
7. If something does not fit, say plainly what, and suggest what to drop or move.

Do not update ledgers from this ritual. Do not create anything without a reply.
