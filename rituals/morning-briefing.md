# Morning briefing

One of the four standing rituals from the Agentic Butler template.

- **Trigger phrases:** "brief me", "morning briefing", "what's today"
- **Default time:** 07:50 in the time zone in `profile.md`. Weekdays, unless they say otherwise.
- **Reads:** today's and recent calendar, mail, chat, meeting notes and files, for each active matter. Each teammate's STATUS line, if any.
- **Writes:** nothing. A same-day re-plan is a proposal until they say "apply".

If this product can schedule a task, offer this time and read the schedule back before turning it on. If a scheduled task cannot see their documents, paste the steps below into the task. If it cannot schedule, run this when they use a trigger phrase. Say which of those is true. Do not claim a morning job you did not set up.

## Steps

Follow the daily-briefing skill.

1. Read today's and recent calendar, mail, chat, meeting notes and files for updates on each active matter. Read each teammate's STATUS line if one exists.
2. Lead with a per-matter board. For each: health, current state, next action, people involved, and what not to lose. Then the day's immovable commitments.
3. Quick ledger pass: what's due today or overdue, and any commitment since yesterday that is not tracked yet. Propose those. Write nothing.
4. If the day changed (a new or moved meeting, a cancellation, an overrun), propose a short adjusted plan that keeps lunch, resets and the end of the day. Change the calendar only after "apply", and only on the Butler calendar once it exists.
5. Add today's weather for their location, unless they asked you to skip it.
6. Deliver it in a calm voice, about 250–450 words. If they are driving or cannot read, 40–90 spoken words, one decision at a time, no links or tables.

Never send mail or chat messages unless they ask. Never repeat private family, health or financial details.
