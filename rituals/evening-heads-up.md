# Evening heads-up

One of the four standing rituals from the Agentic Butler template.

- **Trigger phrases:** "heads-up", "evening heads-up", "what's tomorrow"
- **Default time:** 21:00 in the time zone in `profile.md`.
- **Length:** 6 lines at most. Leave out any line that has nothing to report.
- **Writes:** nothing, except new promises they dictate, which go into `promises.md` or the Open section of the matter file. Those are leads, not ledger rows, until the nightly pass proposes them.

Schedule it only if the product can, and only after they confirm. Otherwise the phrase is the schedule.

## Lines

- Tomorrow's first commitment, and the prep it needs.
- Family items for tomorrow. Times and roles only. No health, no school names.
- Household tasks. If a household teammate exists, use its STATUS line. Do not invent one.
- Commitments due tomorrow, from the Work ledgers.
- Any promise made today that isn't tracked yet. Ask if it should be.

On Day 3 the owner may fold the nightly promise question into this ritual ("Anything you promised someone today?"). Do that only if they say yes, and then retire the separate promise ritual.

Read only. Do not message anyone else.
